# Changelog

What's new in each release of Calendar. The newest is at the top.

## 0.1.2

Widgets
- New Agenda widget: today's date with a + for a new event, then the next 30 days that have something on, grouped by day. Tap an event to open it, or a day to open the calendar there.
- New Month widget: the month on a rounded grid with today circled and a dot for each event. Use the arrows to change months, and tap a day to open it.
- Both widgets use your wallpaper's colours, follow your week start setting, and update when events change here, in another app or from a sync, and at midnight.

Shortcuts
- Long-press the app icon for New event, Search and Today. Each can also be dragged onto the home screen.

## 0.1.1

Look
- The Month, Planner and Week views now sit on a rounded card, like the lists in Contacts and Dialtone, with a thin gap between each day.
- In the Week view, all-day events and the hours are two parts of the same card. The hour labels stay beside it and scroll with it.
- Day headings in Agenda, Planner and search are all in the theme colour, like the headings in Contacts and Dialtone.
- The weekday letters over the month are all the same colour.

## 0.1.0

The first release.

Views
- Month, Week, Agenda and Planner (a month with the picked day's list underneath), switched from the bottom bar. The view you used last opens next time.
- Swipe sideways for other months and weeks, tap the month name to jump to any date, and tap Today (it shows today's date) to come back.
- The Week view shows events hour by hour, side by side where they overlap, with all-day events along the top and a line at the time now.

Events
- Add events with a title, all day or times, repeats (daily, weekly, monthly or yearly), calendar, reminders, place and notes.
- Change or delete just one time of a repeating event, or all of them. Repeat rules made in other apps are kept as they are.
- Search events by their title, place or notes.
- Reminder notifications, which can be turned off if another calendar app already reminds you.

Calendars
- Events are kept in Android's shared calendar storage, so other calendar apps and widgets see them, and calendars from your accounts show up here.
- Show or hide each calendar, and make, rename, recolour or delete calendars on this phone, from Settings or straight from the event editor.

Other apps
- Other apps can open events, add new ones and edit them in Calendar, so it can be the phone's calendar app.
